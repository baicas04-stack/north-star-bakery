Our story

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: give the neighborhood a friendly place to pick up good bread and a treat for the road. We keep our menu focused and make time to help with special requests.

How we choose ingredients

We focus on dependable ingredients and seasonal flavors. When available, we choose flour, dairy, and fruit from regional suppliers. Ask our team about the ingredients in any item.
